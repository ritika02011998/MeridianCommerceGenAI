WARRANTY POLICY — Meridian Commerce

1. Standard Manufacturer Warranty
Most electronics and home appliances sold on Meridian Commerce carry a
manufacturer warranty ranging from 6 months to 24 months, as specified on
each product's page and in its description. Apparel, footwear, and bedding
items do not carry a manufacturer warranty but are covered by standard
return-window quality checks instead.

2. Proof of Purchase
A valid warranty claim requires the original Meridian Commerce invoice or
order confirmation email. If a customer has lost the physical invoice, a
duplicate copy can be retrieved at any time from "My Orders" > "Invoice."
The warranty is NOT voided simply because a paper copy was misplaced — the
digital order record on the customer's account serves as valid proof of
purchase.

3. What Is Covered
- Manufacturing defects present at the time of delivery
- Premature functional failure under normal, intended use
- Faulty components identified within the stated warranty period

4. What Is Not Covered
- Physical damage from drops, spills, or misuse
- Damage from unauthorized repair, disassembly, or modification
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., worn shoe soles, frayed charging cables,
  scratched non-stick coating from metal utensils)
- Water damage on products not rated for water exposure
- Cosmetic issues that do not affect functionality

5. How to File a Warranty Claim
Go to "My Orders" > select the item > "Warranty Claim" > describe the issue
> attach photos or a short video if requested. Our team responds within 48
hours with a repair, replacement, or refund decision. Items may be asked to
be shipped to a service center for inspection on claims involving suspected
misuse.

6. Extended Warranty
Select electronics and home appliances offer a paid Extended Warranty
add-on at checkout, extending coverage by an additional 12 months beyond the
standard manufacturer warranty. Extended Warranty is non-transferable and
tied to the original purchasing account.

7. Warranty vs. Return Window
The return window (see Return & Refund Policy) and the warranty period are
separate. An item outside its return window can still be eligible for a
free repair or replacement under warranty if the fault is a covered defect.
