SHIPPING & DELIVERY POLICY — Meridian Commerce

1. Standard Delivery Timelines
- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune):
  2-4 business days
- Tier-2 cities (Jaipur, Lucknow, Indore, Chandigarh, Kochi, Nagpur, etc.):
  4-6 business days
- Remote or rural pin codes: 6-10 business days

2. Delivery Charges
Orders above ₹999 qualify for free standard shipping. Orders below this
threshold incur a flat ₹79 shipping fee. Expedited shipping (1-2 business
days, metro cities only) is available at checkout for an additional fee.

3. Delayed Delivery Compensation
If a shipment is delayed beyond the promised delivery window by more than 3
business days, the customer is eligible for one of the following, at
Meridian Commerce's discretion:
  a) A full waiver of any shipping fee paid on that order, or
  b) A Meridian Wallet credit of ₹150 for orders above ₹1,500, or
  c) Priority re-shipping at no extra cost if a replacement is requested.
Compensation must be claimed within 30 days of the original expected
delivery date via Support > "Delayed Order."

4. Delays Due to Courier or Force Majeure
Meridian Commerce is not liable for delays caused by courier partner
disruptions, natural disasters, regional strikes, or lockdowns. However,
where such a delay exceeds 7 days beyond the promised window, a partial
goodwill wallet credit will still be offered on request.

5. Order Tracking
Tracking details are emailed and sent via SMS within 24 hours of dispatch.
Live tracking is available under "My Orders" > "Track Package."

6. Address Changes
Delivery address changes are only possible before an order has shipped. Once
an order shows "Shipped" status, the address cannot be changed and the
order must be cancelled and re-placed if the address was incorrect.

7. Failed Delivery Attempts
After 3 failed delivery attempts, the order is returned to our warehouse and
the customer is notified. A refund is issued automatically within 7 business
days unless the customer requests re-delivery within that window.

8. Order Marked "Delivered" But Not Received
If tracking shows "Delivered" but the customer has not received the item,
Support will open a courier investigation. This typically resolves within
5 business days; if the package cannot be located, a full refund or
replacement is issued.

9. International Shipping
Meridian Commerce currently ships only within India. Cross-border shipping
is not supported at this time.
