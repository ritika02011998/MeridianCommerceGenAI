RETURN & REFUND POLICY — Meridian Commerce

1. General Return Window
Most items purchased on Meridian Commerce can be returned within 14 days of
delivery for a full refund or exchange, provided the item is unused, in its
original packaging, and accompanied by the order confirmation or invoice.

2. Category-Specific Rules
- Electronics (headphones, smartwatches, speakers, power banks, chargers,
  earbuds): returnable within 10 days. The item must power on and pass a
  basic functionality check by our returns team. Opened software licenses
  and activated SIM-based devices are non-returnable.
- Laptops, smartphones, tablets, cameras: returnable within 7 days, sealed
  accessories and original packaging required. A 5% restocking fee applies
  if the box has been opened but the device shows no defect.
- Footwear: returnable within 14 days if unworn, with tags and original
  shoebox intact. Shoes returned with visible outdoor wear are rejected.
- Apparel: returnable within 14 days with tags attached and no signs of
  washing or wear.
- Home Goods (blenders, air fryers, cookware, vacuum cleaners, coffee
  makers): returnable within 10 days only if unused and all accessories are
  included. Appliances showing signs of use (food residue, burnt smell,
  scratches on non-stick surfaces) are not eligible for return.
- Bedsheets, storage bins, lamps: returnable within 14 days if unused and
  in original packaging.

3. Non-Returnable Items
- Innerwear, swimwear, and pierced jewelry.
- Digital gift cards and downloadable software licenses.
- Items marked "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at checkout.
- Personal care and grooming devices once the hygiene seal is broken.

4. Condition Requirements
Items must be returned in their original, resalable condition. An appliance
or electronic item that has visibly been used will be rejected during the
returns quality check, regardless of how many days remain in the return
window.

5. How to Initiate a Return
Go to "My Orders" > select the item > "Return or Replace" > choose a reason
> schedule a pickup. A prepaid return label is generated automatically for
eligible returns. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days after the
returned item passes quality inspection at our warehouse.

6. Refund Method
Refunds are issued to the original payment method used at checkout. For
Cash on Delivery (COD) orders, refunds are issued as Meridian Wallet credit
or a direct bank transfer, at the customer's choice.

7. High-Value Return Review
Returns on orders above ₹20,000, or any return flagged by our fraud
detection system (e.g. unusually high return frequency on the account),
are routed to manual review before a refund is approved. Manual review
adds 2-3 business days to the standard refund timeline.

8. Exchanges
Size and color exchanges (footwear and apparel only) are processed as a
combined return-and-reorder and follow the same eligibility windows as
returns above.
