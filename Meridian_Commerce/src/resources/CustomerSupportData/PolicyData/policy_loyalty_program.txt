LOYALTY PROGRAM TERMS — Meridian Rewards

1. Enrollment
Meridian Rewards is free to join and every registered customer is
automatically enrolled from their first completed order. No separate
sign-up is required.

2. Earning Points
Customers earn 1 Meridian Point for every ₹100 spent on eligible orders.
Points are credited once an order is marked "Delivered" and are reversed if
the corresponding item is later returned or refunded.

3. Tiers
- Silver: 0-2,499 points in a rolling 12-month period
- Gold: 2,500-7,499 points — earns 1.25x points on all orders
- Platinum: 7,500+ points — earns 1.5x points on all orders, plus free
  expedited shipping on eligible orders
Tier status is recalculated on the 1st of every month based on the trailing
12 months of activity.

4. Redeeming Points
Points can be redeemed at checkout in blocks of 100 points = ₹10 discount,
up to a maximum of 40% of the order value per transaction. Points cannot be
redeemed on gift cards or combined with certain promotional discount codes,
as shown at checkout.

5. Point Expiry
Points expire 18 months from the date they were earned if unused. Expiry
dates are visible under "My Rewards" > "Points History."

6. Referral Bonus
Referring a friend who completes their first order earns the referring
customer 200 bonus points, credited within 7 days of the referred friend's
first delivered order.

7. Birthday Reward
Members receive a one-time 10%-off voucher (capped at ₹500) during their
birthday month, applicable on orders above ₹999.

8. Program Changes and Termination
Meridian Commerce may modify point-earning rates, tier thresholds, or
redemption rules with 30 days' notice via email. Accounts found to be
abusing the referral or points system (e.g. fraudulent referrals) may have
points forfeited and rewards access suspended.
